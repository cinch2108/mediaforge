      About the crop video to square

      Square video takes up more of the feed than landscape video on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, which usually means more attention. This tool converts any aspect ratio to 1:1. Crop fills the square from the center, Fit letterboxes the whole frame on black or white, and Blur background keeps the full video over a soft blurred fill. Output is H.264 MP4 at 1080, 720 or 480 pixels square.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your video.
      2. 1:1 is preselected. Choose Crop, Fit or Blur background.
      3. Pick the output size. 1080 is standard for social media.
      4. Process and download.

      Tips for the best results

      • For talking-head videos, Crop usually looks best because faces are centered.
      • For slides or screen recordings, use Fit so no text is cut off.
      • A 4:5 ratio (1080×1350) takes up even more feed space on Instagram.

      Settings explained

      How the settings work: the output is sized from the short side you choose and the target aspect ratio, with dimensions kept even for H.264. Fill scales the video up to cover the frame and crops the overflow from the center. Fit scales it down and pads with a solid color. Blur background layers the fitted video over a zoomed, blurred copy of itself.

      Why use Mediaforge for this: other crop video to square sites upload your file to their servers, make you wait in a queue, and often add a watermark or cap the file size unless you pay. Here the work is done by FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, running in a background Web Worker in this tab. Your file is read straight from your disk, processed in memory and handed back as a download, so the counter above always reads 0 bytes uploaded.

      Frequently asked questions

      What resolution is a square video for Instagram?

      1080×1080 pixels is the standard.

      Can I choose which part is cropped?

      Crop currently centers the frame. Use Fit or Blur to keep the whole picture.

      Does it keep the audio?

      Yes, re-encoded to AAC.

      Is it free?

      Yes. It is unlimited and watermark-free.
