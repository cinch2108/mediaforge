      About the mov to mp4 converter

      MOV is Apple's QuickTime container. iPhones and Macs create it by default, but Windows apps, some TVs and many upload forms only accept MP4. Often the video inside a MOV is already H.264 or HEVC, so the fix can be as simple as moving the streams into an MP4 container, which is called remuxing. This tool tries a fast remux first when you enable it and falls back to a full re-encode if the codecs need it, so you always get a file that plays.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your .mov files (AirDrop exports and Photos exports both work).
      2. Keep MP4 as the output format.
      3. Turn on "Fast remux" for near-instant results, or leave it off to re-encode to H.264 for maximum compatibility.
      4. Process, then download your MP4s or the ZIP.

      Tips for the best results

      • iPhone HEVC (H.265) videos are re-encoded to H.264 so they play on older Windows PCs.
      • Re-encoding a 4K iPhone clip in the browser can take a while. Compress to 1080p at the same time with the Compress tool.
      • The MP4 is written with "faststart", so it starts playing immediately when posted online.

      Settings explained

      How the settings work: MP4, MOV and MKV use H.264 video with AAC audio, WebM uses VP8 with Opus, and the audio formats use LAME MP3, AAC, Vorbis, FLAC or 16-bit PCM WAV. The quality setting maps to the encoder's constant rate factor, so the encoder spends bits where the picture needs them instead of using a fixed bitrate. Fast remux copies streams with no re-encoding when the target container supports them, and quietly falls back to a normal conversion when it does not.

      Frequently asked questions

      Why won't my MOV play on Windows?

      It is usually HEVC video inside a MOV container. Converting to MP4 with H.264 makes it play everywhere.

      Is remuxing lossless?

      Yes. Remuxing copies the audio and video streams unchanged, so quality is identical to the original.

      Can I convert a lot of iPhone videos at once?

      Yes. Batch convert as many as your device can handle, then download them all as one ZIP.

      Do you keep a copy of my video?

      No. We never receive the video at all, because it is processed in your browser.
