      About the mp4 to mp3 converter

      Saving a lecture, podcast recording, interview or song from an MP4 file as an MP3 makes it smaller, easier to play in the car and simple to load into any audio app. This page decodes the audio track of your MP4 and encodes it with the LAME MP3 encoder, all inside your browser. Because nothing is uploaded, converting a two-hour recording takes about as long as your computer needs to encode it, with no upload or download time on top.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your MP4 files onto the upload box. Nothing is actually uploaded; the files are only read locally.
      2. Check that MP3 is selected as the output format.
      3. Choose a bitrate: 192 kbps is great for music, 128 kbps is plenty for speech, and 320 kbps is the maximum.
      4. Click Process and download your MP3s one at a time or as a single ZIP.

      Tips for the best results

      • For voice-only recordings, 96–128 kbps keeps files tiny without audible loss.
      • Want the original audio with no re-encoding at all? Use Extract Audio and pick "Original".
      • The same page works for MOV, MKV, WebM and AVI videos too.

      Settings explained

      How the settings work: MP4, MOV and MKV use H.264 video with AAC audio, WebM uses VP8 with Opus, and the audio formats use LAME MP3, AAC, Vorbis, FLAC or 16-bit PCM WAV. The quality setting maps to the encoder's constant rate factor, so the encoder spends bits where the picture needs them instead of using a fixed bitrate. Fast remux copies streams with no re-encoding when the target container supports them, and quietly falls back to a normal conversion when it does not.

      Why use Mediaforge for this: other mp4 to mp3 converter sites upload your file to their servers, make you wait in a queue, and often add a watermark or cap the file size unless you pay.       Frequently asked questions

      Does converting MP4 to MP3 reduce quality?

      MP3 is a lossy format, so audio is re-encoded once. At 192 kbps or higher the difference is inaudible for almost everyone.

      Can I convert many MP4 files at once?

      Yes. Drop a whole folder and they are processed one after another. When they finish, download them all as a ZIP.

      Is it safe to convert private recordings here?

      Yes. The conversion runs inside your browser tab and the file is never sent anywhere, so it is safe even for confidential meetings.

      Does it work on iPhone and Android?

      Yes, in Safari and Chrome. Phones have less memory, so very long videos work best on a computer.
