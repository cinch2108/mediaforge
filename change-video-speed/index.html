      About the change video speed

      Speeding up a long tutorial, making a punchy time-lapse from a slow clip, or slowing down a skateboard trick all come down to changing playback speed. This tool rescales the video timestamps and time-stretches the audio with FFmpeg's atempo filter, which keeps voices at their natural pitch instead of sounding like chipmunks. Choose any speed from 0.25× to 4×. Audio files work too, which is useful for podcasts and voice notes.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your video or audio files.
      2. Choose a speed: below 1× slows down, above 1× speeds up.
      3. Press Process. The new duration is shown as each file finishes.
      4. Download the result.

      Tips for the best results

      • 2× is great for cooking or crafting clips, and 4× gives a quick time-lapse.
      • Slowing below 0.5× repeats frames, so for true slow motion, record at 60–240 fps.
      • Combine with Trim to speed up only part of a clip, then merge the parts.

      Settings explained

      How the settings work: video speed is changed by rescaling the presentation timestamps (setpts), and audio is time-stretched with chained atempo filters, so pitch stays natural at every speed. The output duration is the original divided by the speed factor.

      Why use Mediaforge for this: other change video speed sites upload your file to their servers, make you wait in a queue, and often add a watermark or cap the file size unless you pay. Here the work is done by FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, running in a background Web Worker in this tab. Your file is read straight from your disk, processed in memory and handed back as a download, so the counter above always reads 0 bytes uploaded.

      Frequently asked questions

      Does it keep the audio pitch?

      Yes. The audio is time-stretched with pitch preserved.

      Can I change speed of an MP3?

      Yes. Audio files are supported.

      What is the maximum speed?

      4×, and the minimum is 0.25×.

      Is there a length limit?

      No, only device memory.
