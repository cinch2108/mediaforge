      About the wav to mp3

      WAV is uncompressed, so a three-minute song takes about 30 MB, while the same song as a 192 kbps MP3 is about 4 MB. Converting is the easiest way to share recordings from a DAW, field recorder or voice recorder, or to fit a music library on a phone. This converter uses the LAME encoder at your chosen bitrate. Since WAV files are big, skipping the upload step that other sites require saves a lot of time.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your .wav files or a folder of them.
      2. MP3 is preselected. Choose 128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps.
      3. Process.
      4. Download the MP3s or a ZIP.

      Tips for the best results

      • Use 320 kbps for final music masters and 128 kbps for spoken word.
      • Keep the original WAVs if you might edit again, because MP3 is lossy.
      • 24-bit and 32-bit float WAVs are supported and converted correctly.

      Settings explained

      How the settings work: MP4, MOV and MKV use H.264 video with AAC audio, WebM uses VP8 with Opus, and the audio formats use LAME MP3, AAC, Vorbis, FLAC or 16-bit PCM WAV. The quality setting maps to the encoder's constant rate factor, so the encoder spends bits where the picture needs them instead of using a fixed bitrate. Fast remux copies streams with no re-encoding when the target container supports them, and quietly falls back to a normal conversion when it does not.

      Why use Mediaforge for this: other wav to mp3 sites upload your file to their servers, make you wait in a queue, and often add a watermark or cap the file size unless you pay. Here the work is done by FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, running in a background Web Worker in this tab. Your file is read straight from your disk, processed in memory and handed back as a download, so the counter above always reads 0 bytes uploaded.

      Frequently asked questions

      How much smaller is MP3 than WAV?

      At 192 kbps, about 7× smaller. At 128 kbps, about 11× smaller.

      Will I hear a difference?

      At 192 kbps or above, almost nobody can tell in normal listening.

      Can I convert 1 GB WAV files?

      Yes, if your device has the memory. There is no artificial cap.

      Is it free?

      Yes. It is unlimited and requires no account.
