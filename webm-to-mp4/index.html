      About the webm to mp4 converter

      WebM files come from browser screen recorders, Chrome's MediaRecorder, and many web downloads. They use VP8 or VP9 video with Opus or Vorbis audio, which PowerPoint, iMovie, WhatsApp and many phones refuse to play. Converting to MP4 re-encodes the picture to H.264 and the sound to AAC, the pairing every device supports. Recordings made in a browser often have no duration stored in the file, and converting fixes that too, so seeking works properly.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your .webm files on the box above.
      2. Make sure MP4 is selected.
      3. Choose High quality for screen recordings with small text, or Balanced for camera footage.
      4. Process and download.

      Tips for the best results

      • Screen recordings compress very well. Balanced quality usually halves the size without blurring text.
      • For a looping preview, convert to GIF instead.
      • You can record your screen on this site with the Screen Recorder and convert the result right away.

      Settings explained

      How the settings work: MP4, MOV and MKV use H.264 video with AAC audio, WebM uses VP8 with Opus, and the audio formats use LAME MP3, AAC, Vorbis, FLAC or 16-bit PCM WAV. The quality setting maps to the encoder's constant rate factor, so the encoder spends bits where the picture needs them instead of using a fixed bitrate. Fast remux copies streams with no re-encoding when the target container supports them, and quietly falls back to a normal conversion when it does not.

      Why use Mediaforge for this: other webm to mp4 converter sites upload your file to their servers, make you wait in a queue, and often add a watermark or cap the file size unless you pay.       Frequently asked questions

      Why convert WebM to MP4?

      MP4 with H.264 plays in more places, including iOS, PowerPoint, most video editors and social apps.

      Will the audio stay in sync?

      Yes. Timestamps are rebuilt during the conversion, which also repairs the missing duration in browser recordings.

      How big can my WebM be?

      Up to about 2 GB per file, which is the browser memory ceiling. There is no artificial cap.

      Is a WebM to MP4 conversion slow in the browser?

      It runs at roughly 0.5–3× real time for 1080p, depending on your CPU, and you skip the upload and download entirely.
