      About the resize video for tiktok, reels & shorts

      TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are all designed for 9:16 vertical video at 1080×1920. If you post a landscape clip as-is, the platform shrinks it into a thin strip. This tool offers three ways to reframe. Fill crops to the center so the video fills the screen, Fit adds bars, and Blur background places your full video over a blurred, zoomed copy of itself, the polished look creators use. It is preset to blurred background at 1080p.

      How to use it

      1. Drop your landscape or square video.
      2. 9:16 is preselected. Choose Fill, Fit or Blur background.
      3. Keep 1080 for full HD, or pick 720 for faster processing.
      4. Process and upload the MP4 to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

      Tips for the best results

      • Blur background keeps everything in frame, which is best for gameplay and screen recordings.
      • Fill (crop) is best when the action is in the center of the frame.
      • Keep important text away from the bottom 20% of the frame, where captions and buttons cover it.

      Settings explained

      How the settings work: the output is sized from the short side you choose and the target aspect ratio, with dimensions kept even for H.264. Fill scales the video up to cover the frame and crops the overflow from the center. Fit scales it down and pads with a solid color. Blur background layers the fitted video over a zoomed, blurred copy of itself.

      Why use Mediaforge for this: other resize video for tiktok, reels & shorts sites upload your file to their servers, make you wait in a queue, and often add a watermark or cap the file size unless you pay. Here the work is done by FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, running in a background Web Worker in this tab. Your file is read straight from your disk, processed in memory and handed back as a download, so the counter above always reads 0 bytes uploaded.

      Frequently asked questions

      What size should TikTok videos be?

      1080×1920 pixels (9:16). This tool outputs exactly that at the 1080 setting.

      Does it work for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts?

      Yes. All three use 9:16 vertical video.

      Is there a watermark?

      No, never. Platforms can down-rank videos with other apps' watermarks, so a clean export helps.

      Can I do square or 4:5 instead?

      Yes. Choose 1:1 or 4:5 in the ratio picker.
